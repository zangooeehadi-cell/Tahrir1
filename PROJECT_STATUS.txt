HADI — pitch-safe tahrir processing revision
Implemented:
- Zero-centered custom and built-in pitch contours to reduce sustained pitch bias.
- Conservative pitch excursion cap of 35 cents, scaled by Auto Tahrir and Depth.
- Removed steady-state parallel dry/pitch-shift blend to avoid simultaneous competing pitches.
- Kept formant reference neutral instead of modulating it with the rough F0 estimate.
- Preserved latency-aligned dry path for inactive periods.

Still unvalidated:
- Audio quality and note stability must be tested with real vocal recordings.
- F0 tracking remains a basic monophonic autocorrelation gate.
- No robust vibrato/tahrir classifier or independent enhancement of existing tahrir.
- No validated artist-specific presets or voice imitation.
- Cubase host compatibility, CPU use and latency need practical testing.
